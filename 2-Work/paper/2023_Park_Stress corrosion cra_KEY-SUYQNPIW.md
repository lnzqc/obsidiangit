> [!quote]- <center>Abstract</center>
> 
> The stress corrosion cracking during the operation of the internal combustion hot blast stove was analysed. The computational fluid dynamics and finite element analysis models were established to analyse the temperature, stress and other variables related to the condensation of the water and acids. The corrosion characteristics of condensation of acid and the stress corrosion cracking of the metallic shell of the hot blast stove during the operation were predicted by applying the fluid temperature and mapping it to the solid temperature. The stress corrosion cracking surface mobility mechanism was adopted and modified with a weight concept to consider the effect of the acid condensation and its concentration. The regions that have higher crack propagation rates were analysed. The influence of the increase in the blast temperature on the crack propagation rate was studied with the increase in the blast temperature by 45 K and 90 K from the reference blast temperature. The maximum temperature of the refractory linings was 1847 K in the on-gas period, and the maximum change in the shell temperature was 5.2 K when the blast temperature was increased by 90 K. The maximum crack propagation rate for the reference blast temperature was evaluated as 7.61×10−7 m/s. The maximum value of the crack propagation rate was increased by 16.7% when the blast temperature increased by 90 K. The conical region was found to have higher crack propagation rates, which means that the conical region should be the region of interest for managing the internal combustion hot blast stoves.

> 🎯 研究问题:: 分析内燃式热风炉壳体在操作过程中的应力腐蚀裂纹（SCC）行为。
> 
> 🔎 研究背景:: 长期运行的热风炉（HBS）中，应力腐蚀裂纹（SCC）是导致结构失效的主要原因之一，尤其是在暴露于酸性环境中时。SCC过程尚未完全阐明，存在多种理论预测SCC增长率以解释结构崩溃案例。
> 
> 🚀 研究方法:: 结合计算流体动力学（CFD）和有限元分析（FEA）模型，分析温度、应力和其他与水和酸冷凝相关的变量。采用并修改了应力腐蚀裂纹表面流动性机制（SMM），引入权重概念以考虑酸冷凝及其浓度的影响。
> 
> 🐔 研究思路:: 通过CFD模型预测水和硝酸、硫酸的冷凝率，利用FEA计算金属壳体的热机械应力，并结合权重值概念分析HBS的易损点和SCC。
> 
> 📺 主要内容:: 研究了热风炉在不同爆破温度下的应力腐蚀裂纹行为，分析了爆破温度对裂纹扩展速率的影响，并确定了裂纹扩展速率较高的区域。
> 
> 🎉 研究结论:: 研究发现，不考虑权重因素时，裂纹扩展速率在上燃烧室的圆锥区域最大。水、硫酸和硝酸的沉积率在HBS的下部更多。爆破温度的增加对壳体温度影响较小，但会导致最大裂纹扩展速率增加16.7%。因此，从安全管理的角度来看，监测圆锥区域非常重要。
> 
> 🗝️ 创新点:: 将SCC表面流动性机制（SMM）修改为适用于工业问题的应力腐蚀裂纹的理论预测，引入基于水和酸冷凝率的权重计算，以考虑腐蚀对裂纹扩展速率的影响。
> 
> 💩 研究局限:: 研究中未考虑焊接残余应力的影响，未来的研究可以包括应用改进的层次分析过程（AHP）方法和SCC过程的现象学关系，以及考虑焊接残余应力对SCC的影响。
> 
> 🐾 研究展望:: 未来的工作建议包括应用改进的AHP方法和SCC过程的现象学关系，包括应用损伤模型来评估损伤演变，以及考虑焊接残余应力对SCC的影响。
